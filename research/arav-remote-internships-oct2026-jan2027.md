## How to use the shortlist

1. Apply first to 5–8 roles where you can show relevant work. Include a working demo and GitHub link, even if you have no previous internship.
2. Put “India · 20–30 hours/week · available [exact dates]” near the top of each application. Ask for agreement on these terms before doing a substantial assignment.
3. For agent roles, show one repository with tool calling, a working API integration and a small evaluation set. Describe only what you personally built.
4. For a board lead, follow through to the employer's application and recheck the role before submitting. Do not rely on the board's salary or deadline alone.
5. If available only until January, focus on 1–3 month roles. Several listings require 6 months; do not promise that unless you can continue.

For your first experience, I would prioritize an AI engineering internship or a technical founder's-office internship over a CTO/cofounder position. The cofounder listings above expect production ownership or substantial experience and may offer equity without immediate salary.
